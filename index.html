      What it converts

      Images
      HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, TIFF, BMP, SVG and ICO to JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, TIFF, BMP, ICO or PDF. Resize and set quality while you’re at it.
      PDF
      Pages to JPG or PNG, text to Word or plain text, split into single pages, or merge PDFs and photos into one file.
      Documents
      Word (.docx), Markdown, HTML and plain text to PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown or text.
      Spreadsheets and data
      Excel, OpenDocument, CSV, TSV, JSON, YAML and XML to each other. Every sheet is kept.
      Audio
      MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, Ogg and Opus to MP3, WAV, M4A or Ogg.
      Video
      MP4, MOV, WebM and MKV to MP4, WebM, MOV or MKV, a short GIF, or just the sound as MP3, WAV or M4A.
      ZIP
      Open a ZIP to get the files out, or pack any files into one.

      Your files stay on this device

      Most converters upload your file to a server, convert it there, and send it back. Permute does the whole job inside this browser tab, so there is nothing to upload and nothing that could be kept.

      You can check this: once the page has loaded, turn off Wi‑Fi and keep converting. Every library Permute uses is served from this site, and the page’s security policy blocks it from contacting anywhere else.
